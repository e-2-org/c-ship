1. Put a funnel type of screen on your chimney, or install a 6 inch sock
from the screen, so that bats will crawl out of the chimney thru the
funnel or sock but be unable to get back in.

2. Start a fairly small fire with the flue wide open, then add enough
green tree leaves (tree because the smoke from most tree leaves appears to
be fairly strong) to smoke them out, repeat as needed until they are all
gone, after they are gone just be sure to screen ALL of your openings down
to a size smaller than bats can navigate... as they will be looking for a
new home.

3. Another method is just to wait for cold weather, then when the bats
leave to their winter accomendations, just screen up the chimney and they
will look elsewhere to live when they return after the winter is over.
 

  In any rural area you need to be sure to close or screen any openings,
otherwise they will ALWAYS be investigated by various fauna looking for
safe havens.
