Each villa has been carefully designed in the true Bahamian spirit by of one the country's most esteemed architects. Palm-frond thatched roofs and white-washed outer walls create a natural fusion with the island's habitat. High ceilings and pristine architecture provide a spacious and airy feel. The villas are cooled by ceiling fans, and open-louvred verandas promote natural ventilation with the additional cooling effect of the sea breeze. Large shuttered picture windows open up onto the most astonishing views over the Atlantic or into the deep green of the palm forest. A wrap-around private terrace extends off each villa with glass doors opening out onto both a sundeck and shaded area, making this the perfect place to linger in the hotter hours of the afternoon. And finally, next to each private villa is a palm-tied hammock, fit for two.

Each villa offers between 1000-1800 square feet of private living space including a large, open-plan living room, a fully-fitted kitchen area, the choice of one or two bedrooms, a bathroom with jacuzzi, bath and shower, and a private terrace and sundeck. For those desiring the epitome of luxury, the two-bedroom deluxe villa benefits from an extra en-suite bathroom, an expansive deck with daybed and plunge pool, an oversized bathroom with double bath, and a small study with rooftop terrace and garden.
