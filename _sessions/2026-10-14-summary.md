# container-ship: session summary

14 October 2026, working with Claude Code on the `jekyll` branch, following on from the conversion on 8 October ([2026-10-08-summary.md](2026-10-08-summary.md)). The full conversation is in [2026-10-14-transcript.md](2026-10-14-transcript.md).

## Aim

Fix errors spotted in the converted site, and carry on modernising it: more of the original lettering as text, links to the artworks' new repositories, and a page for Bird Island.

## What was done

**The deck** (`section.html`, `_includes/deck.html`, `_data/deck.yml`, `css/partials/deck.css`, `js/deck.js`):

- Room pictures are transparent GIFs, so `indexb.gif` showed through them. The panel's own image is now hidden while a room or container is lit, as the original swapped the image.
- The ship takes two thirds of the width, with the quay right-aligned in the rest at the same scale. Below 60em the quay is hidden and the ship stops shrinking (it stays the size it was at the breakpoint) and scrolls sideways.
- The deck's room and container links had the classes `room` and `container`; `.room` is also the room pages' layout, which padded the hotspots and knocked the ship out of line. They are now `deck-room` and `deck-container`.
- On a touch screen, the first tap on a room or container lights it up and shows its picture; a second tap (on it or its picture) follows the link.
- The pictures in the panels link where their room or container does. With a mouse, the last one pointed at stays lit (after resting on it for 150ms) until another is, or the pointer leaves the ship, so the picture can be reached.
- Help and the introduction open in overlays (`<dialog>`) instead of pages; `help.html` and `intro.html` still exist, sharing their text with the overlays through `_includes/help.html` and `_includes/intro.html`. The red and orange dots are now text links ("help", "intro") in the lettering font, in the colour of their dot and white on hover.
- The credit line sits at the foot of the window.

**The lettering font.** The titles, menus and names were images of a 1990s monospaced, unicase typeface that couldn't be identified. Major Mono Display was tried and didn't match, so a font was made from the images: `fonts/c-ship-lettering.woff2` ("C-Ship Lettering"). The letters in the artists' name images (13px a letter) were cut out, smoothed and traced; p, k, x, j, q, z, old-style figures and punctuation were drawn on the same grid and traced the same way. The scripts and how to rebuild it are in `_fonts/` (README there). It's used, through the `lettering` class, for:

- work titles and artist names (the bracket beside the name is CSS)
- room headings, which now show the images' wording ("programme", "ideas and intentions" ...), with the room name for screen readers
- the room menu, whose double-triangle arrows are CSS, with the filled triangle sliding over the outline one on hover, as in the original
- "back to artists and index", "index page", "about the artist" and "back", now text links with CSS dots; on the white pages they are the colour of their dot, black on hover
- the bridge's project titles

**The bridge.** Artist names are plain text links (orange, no underline) to the artist pages; project titles, without their quotes, are `h3` headings linking to the project pages, under an `h2` "Artists' projects". The front matter has `about` and `work` for each project.

**Rooms.** The menu column is as wide as the room picture (220px), so the text lines up under the heading; on small screens the header stacks under the picture with the same 1em margin as the text; room headings are 1.2em.

**Bird Island.** A project page, `_works/acjk.html`, with text from the bird-island README, Peter Moore as Technical Director, and a launch picture (`images/imljk.gif`) made from the island photo in that repo. The deck's container and the bridge link to it.

**Artworks' new homes.** The launch links on the project and artist pages point to each artwork's own repository on the e-2 site: `/adelaide/`, `/error404/`, `/tome/tome.html` (Tome has no index page), `/ahouse/`, `/nowhere/`, `/pigstails/` and `/bird-island/`. Links from the site root are no longer given the `/c-ship` prefix. Cruisin' had no link and still hasn't.

"Viewing requirements" were taken out of every project's credits.
