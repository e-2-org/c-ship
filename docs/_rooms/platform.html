Selected pieces submitted to the comments book will be reproduced here with the intention of generating an ongoing debate about C-ship, electronic media and contemporary art in general.

As an adjunct to the art works themselves Container-ship will also periodically commission a writer connected either with contemporary art or electronic media to produce an article that bears upon some of the concerns dealt with within the artists work which will also be housed here.

The first piece of writing, commissioned for the launch, will be an article by James Gaywood.
