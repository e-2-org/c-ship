Viewing the commission as a site-specific project, Collins has inhabited the ship with a chat-space which roams the decks. Cruisin' explores issues of interaction in digital space and, by its addictive nature, reveals how in this dislocated realm we yearn human interaction.

Unfortunately, the server on which Cruisin' was installed was hacked and the piece lost.
