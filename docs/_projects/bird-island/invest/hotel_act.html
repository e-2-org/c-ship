Hotel Encouragement Act

This incentive provides for duty-free entry of approved construction materials, furnishings, and fixtures for development in the Bahamas. The act reduces the demand on cash flow for small-scale developers and encourages regular property renovations. Recent amendments to the act have reduced the number of rooms required for new developments to qualify into five rooms for resorts in the Family Islands. This reduction is expected to stimulate an expansion of small, eco-sensitive hotels and guest houses. Total new construction and renovation programmes approved under this act exceeds US$200 million since January 1993.
