I used to be indifferent to cats, but then, one year during deer season this poor little straggly longhaired kitten came into the barn as I was quartering my buck.  I thought she was after the deer, so I gave her a kidney and a hunk of tallow. I wanted to take her home. Her loyalty and affection is much truer than that of most people I've known, and I'd defend her to my death if it ever came to it.
