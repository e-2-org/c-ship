Container-ship is built around the metaphor of a cargo boat. To access artist's work, pass the cursor over the boxes on the deck of the ship - this reveals a thumbnail image of each project. Clicking on this box swill open the project in the same browser window - use your browser's back button when you have finished viewing the piece.

Other areas containing more information about c-ship are found towards the rear of the boat.

Best viewed with: Netscape Navigator 3 or above.

Further details of how best to view the individual artist's pieces are to be found on the title page for each piece. Please e-mail us if you would like further information about viewing the work.
