Halloween Origami
The Models: Cats:
              Fred Rohm's Halloween Cat
                    in Best of Origami, by Samuel Randlett.
                    [Yes, this model requires cutting.]
              Toshie Takahama's Cat
                    in Classic Origami, by Paul Jackson
              Teruo Tsuji's Cat
                    in ORU #11, photo page 46, diagrams pages 128-130. [This is a two piece model and it has only one front leg.]
Bats:
              Michael LaFosse's Happy, Good Luck Bat
                    1995 OUSA Convention book (out of print :-( :-( )
                    his video Happy, Good Luck Bat & Horseshoe Crab available from Origamido
                    Origami List FTP Archive
                    Alex Barber's Website
              Tom Hull's Flapping Bat
                    On Tom Hull's Website
Witches:
              Paul Jackson's Witch on a Broomstick
                    in Festive Folding, by Paul Jackson.
                    (this model needs a bit of tweaking, but is good as is too, and pretty simple/fast to fold)
              Miyajima Noboru's Witch on a Broomstick
                    in Origami Tanteidan 4th Convention Book (pages 132-140)
                    complex and cool looking (saw one folded by a co-worker and co-folder, haven't folded it myself yet)
              David Brill's Halloween Witch
                    in Brilliant Origami, by David Brill.
                    (this model is three pieces and requires glue.)
              Kunihiko Kasahara's Witch on a Broomstick
                    in Creative Origami, by Kunihiko Kasahara
              Steve and Megumi Biddle's Flying Witch
                    in Horrorgami, by Steve and Megumi Biddle
                    (This book has been panned on the origami email list.)
              Jill Smolinski's Flying Witch
                    in Scary Origami, by Jill Smolinksi
                    (This book has also been panned on the origami email list.)
              Robert Harbin's Welch Witch
                    in The Flapping Bird, by Samuel and Jean Randlett.
              Robert Neale's Wizard and Witch
                    in Mythical Beings, by Jay Ansill
       Ghosts:
              Robert Neale's Ghost
                    in The Flapping Bird, by Samuel and Jean Randlett.
       Headless Man:
              Robert Neale's Headless Man
                    in The Flapping Bird, by Samuel and Jean Randlett.
       Pumpkins:
              Sy Chen's Pumpkin Face
                    on Sy Chen's Website.
                    Sy says that you can add lower teeth by adding an extra pleat in step 7.
              Anita F. Barbour's Pumpkin
                    on Perry Bailey's Website
       Devils/Demons:
              Jun Maekawa's Demon
                    in Viva Origami [out of print :-(].
       Skeletons:
              Marc Kirschenbaum's Skeleton
                    on the Origami List FTP Site.
